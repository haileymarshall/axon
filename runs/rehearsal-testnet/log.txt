The gene that causes Laing distal myopathy (LDM) is located on chromosome 19q24. It encodes for the protein alpha-actinin-3 (AAN). The AAN gene has two copies: one from each parent. In individuals with LDM, there is an extra copy of the AAN gene, resulting in a condition called autosomal dominant inheritance. This means that if one child inherits the mutated gene from both parents, they will also have the condition.
